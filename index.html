We can prove what the media did

Through Nzika Centre for Education and Communication and our partnership with ReelAnalytics of Kenya, we run media monitoring infrastructure inside Malawi. Coverage reporting is measured, not estimated.

The network took twenty years

Our contacts across Malawi's media houses were built over more than two decades of professional practice. Placement is a phone call, not a cold pitch.
